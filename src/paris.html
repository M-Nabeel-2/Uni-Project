Top Tourist Places in Paris

Eiffel Tower

The iconic iron tower and symbol of Paris, offering panoramic views from its upper decks.

Louvre Museum

Home to the Mona Lisa, this world-famous museum features thousands of artworks across history.

Notre-Dame Cathedral

A masterpiece of French Gothic architecture, currently being restored after the 2019 fire.

Montmartre & Sacré-Cœur

A historic hilltop district known for its artistic heritage, charming streets, and the white-domed basilica.

Palace of Versailles

A royal château just outside Paris, famous for its opulent rooms and expansive gardens.

Seine River Cruise

A scenic boat ride along the Seine offering views of major landmarks including the Eiffel Tower and Notre-Dame.
