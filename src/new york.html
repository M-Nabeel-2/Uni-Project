Top Tourist Places in New York

Statue of Liberty

America's symbol of freedom and democracy, located on Liberty Island and accessible by ferry.

Times Square

A bustling commercial hub known for bright billboards, Broadway theaters, and New Year's Eve ball drop.

Central Park

A vast green oasis in Manhattan offering walking trails, lakes, zoos, and peaceful escapes from city life.

Empire State Building

An iconic skyscraper with observation decks offering stunning panoramic views of New York City.

Brooklyn Bridge

A historic suspension bridge connecting Manhattan and Brooklyn, popular for walking and photography.

Metropolitan Museum of Art

One of the largest and most prestigious museums in the world, featuring art from ancient to modern times.
